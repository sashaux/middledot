Special characters, one click away: « » – — · → ≠ ± ©. Copy instantly and arrange your own set.

Description
middledot keeps the characters missing from your keyboard a click away.

Open the popup, click a character — it’s already in your clipboard. Paste it anywhere.

What’s inside
• Quotes, apostrophe and dashes: « » „ “ ” ’ – —
• Arrows: ← → ↑ ↓
• Math: ~ ≈ ≠ ≤ ≥ × − ± °
• And more: $ € • · © ツ

Make it yours
• Click the pencil to edit the grid
• Click any cell and type a character — emoji too
• Drag characters to rearrange them
• Reset to the default set with two clicks
Your layout is saved in your browser.

Small details
• Hover over a character to see its name and Unicode code point, so an en dash never passes for a minus
• Works from the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to copy
• Light and dark themes follow your system

No accounts, no tracking, no network requests. The only permission it asks for is writing to the clipboard.
